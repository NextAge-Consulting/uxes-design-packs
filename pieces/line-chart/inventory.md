| `line-chart` | A line chart: a value over a run of points, series in the chart colours in a fixed order with a sixth folding into "Other", a missing value breaking the line, bottom labels thinned to fit; a crosshair, by pointer or arrow keys, reads out every series at its point; a legend from two series, and a table for screen readers.
